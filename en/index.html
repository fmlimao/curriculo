Success Stories

AI Sales Ecosystem

Escale

Flagship project

My largest and most ambitious project. Escale ran its automated sales ecosystem on third-party systems — it worked, but bringing each new client into production took around 60 days and required updating several systems, creating so much friction that some clients churned before going live. We built our own ecosystem: around 20 new systems, all built 100% with AI, fully architected and integrated, with client onboarding now happening from a single dashboard.

  • The AI automatically generates and tests the sales agents' prompts and suggests A/B test plans.
  • It supports the client in building the knowledge base.
  • It analyzes 100% of conversations, message by message — detecting when a lead opts for the product and even recognizing the lead's mood.
  • It builds the dashboards and organizes each client's sales funnel.

60 days → 4 hours to bring a new client's environment into production

2–3 / year → 3 / month in new client acquisition pace
